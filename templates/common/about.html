About Holdwise

Holdwise is a personal investment portfolio tracker. Create portfolios, add holdings by ticker symbol and see what each position is worth today, how much you have gained or lost, and how your money is spread across your investments.

Features

  • Live prices for stocks, ETFs and crypto from Yahoo Finance
  • Profit and loss per holding and per portfolio
  • A cash reserve per portfolio: deposits, withdrawals and sale proceeds
  • Watchlists for assets you are keeping an eye on

Prices may be delayed. Holdwise is a tracking tool, not investment advice.
